Our *Story*

Shinrai started in a bedroom studio with two things: a shared folder of unreleased funk loops and a running list of labels that had gone quiet on friends' demos. So we built the label we wished existed — **fast replies, honest splits, and promotion that actually moves the needle.** No shelved masters, no fine print.

Direct Communication

You hear from a person, not a form-letter, within two weeks of submitting.

Fair Splits

80/20 in the artist's favor, every release, no exceptions or hidden fees.

Real Promotion

Playlist pitching, social push, and cover art — not just a distribution link.

Run by a small team of producers and A&R who still make music themselves — we sign what we'd want in our own rotation.
